# AMV-01 — Master Systems Engineering Project Plan

**Project:** Autonomous Mini-Vehicle (AMV-01)  
**Project Type:** Personal Systems & Requirements Engineering Portfolio Project  
**Status:** In Development  
**Started:** September 2026  
**Target Completion:** January 2027  

---

# Project Goal

Develop a functional autonomous mini-vehicle while applying and
documenting a structured Systems Engineering and Requirements
Engineering lifecycle.

The primary focus of the project is Systems Engineering and Requirements
Engineering.

The physical vehicle, embedded electronics, sensors, software, LiDAR,
CAN communication, and mechanical prototype will be used to implement
and verify the system defined through the engineering process.

---
